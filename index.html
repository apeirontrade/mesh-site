How it works

1

Read freely

Anyone can browse the board, with no wallet needed.

2

Own an NFD to post

Connect the wallet that holds your .algo name. That's your login, with no form and no password.

3

Post for a fraction of a cent

Each post costs about the same as an Algorand transaction, so spam gets expensive.

4

Like free, tip directly

Reactions are free. Tips go 100% to the post's author.

Post-quantum by design

  • Every post

    Signed with ML-DSA, a post-quantum signature tied to your name, so nobody can forge a post from you, even with a future quantum computer.

  • Every hour

    A fingerprint of new posts is stamped on Algorand by mesh's Falcon post-quantum account, so nobody can silently edit or backdate history, including us.

  • Optional

    Fully quantum-safe badge: owners who move their name to an Algorand Falcon account get end-to-end post-quantum protection.

  • Next

    Private messages with post-quantum encryption (ML-KEM-768 + X25519) between any two names.

Honest status

This is a design preview, not a working product yet. Nothing can be posted today. The platform is being built and tested on Algorand testnet first.

Prices shown are launch targets and may change before launch. The cryptography code will need an independent security review before anyone relies on it.
